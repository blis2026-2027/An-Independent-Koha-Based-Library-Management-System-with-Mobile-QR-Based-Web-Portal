About NORSU Siaton Campus Library

Head of the Library: Mr. Charlton M. Raga

History of Siaton Campus Library

In 1997, through SB Resolution headed by municipal mayor Teopisto F. Yap, Siaton Community College was established under Mr. Licerio T. Napao at the Old District Engineering Office in Brgy. 3, Poblacion. The library began with two shelves and fewer than 100 donated books, led by Eleuterio Futalan.

By 1991, the school transferred to the Municipal Motorpool Building (its current location). By virtue of Republic Act No. 9299, CVPC was converted into Negros Oriental State University (NORSU), integrating Siaton Community College as NORSU Siaton Campus under University President Dr. Henry A. Sojor.

Today, the library holds 7,000 books, 10 periodical subscriptions, 20 computer stations, and online e-journal/e-book subscriptions.

Library Mission

To make available and deliver information resources in all formats to members of the academic community, ensuring knowledge preservation through collection building, resource access, and academic program support.

Official Library Policies & Borrowing Rules

Established rules for campus students, faculty, and external visitors

General House Rules

  • Entry & ID: Open to all students and faculty upon presenting a valid ID card.
  • Inspection: Personal bags and belongings must be presented for exit inspection.
  • Environment: Quiet atmosphere and cleanliness must be observed at all times.
  • External Researchers: In-house access permitted upon prior clearance from head librarian. Physical borrowing is prohibited; requested materials are provided via scanned copies sent to email or Messenger.

Borrowing & Fines Policy

  • Novels & Fiction Collection: Allowed for home borrowing for up to one (1) week.
  • Textbooks & Academic References: Physical borrowing is not permitted for students or faculty. Digital chapter scans are provided.
  • Compassionate Fine-Free Policy: No overdue fines are charged to encourage student reading and research engagement.
